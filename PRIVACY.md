# Curie privacy notice

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Curie has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no Curie server. Your data stays on your device unless you use one of the features below.

## What stays on your device

- Lessons, notes, quiz history, study days, settings and the assistant conversation.
- In the desktop and Android apps they are files in Curie's app data folder; on the web they are in your browser's storage.
- API keys you enter for AI providers. They are stored separately from your settings, never included in exported lessons, and sent only to the provider they belong to.

## When Curie uses the network

| Feature | What is sent, and to whom |
|---|---|
| **Study assistant** | Your messages, the conversation so far, and what the assistant needs to answer: matching passages from your notes, and the results of steps it takes (which can include note text, lesson text and a summary of your progress). This goes to the AI provider you choose in Settings: Anthropic (Claude), an OpenAI-compatible service, or an Ollama server you run. Each provider's own privacy terms apply. With Ollama, data stays on hardware you control. |
| **Photos of notes** | The photo, reduced in size, is sent to your AI provider to be transcribed. PDFs and text files are read on your device. |
| **Explore** | Requests to GitHub to list and download lesson files from the sources you've added. |
| **Lessons with web media** | Images or videos that a lesson links from the web are loaded from those sites. |

## Your controls

- Turn off automatic note excerpts, or the assistant's tools, in Settings → AI assistant.
- Remove an API key by clearing its field.
- Delete notes individually, or clear notes, quiz history and the chat in Settings → Library & data.
- Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes everything Curie stored.
